# Welcome to MD Brain

MD Brain is a local-first, deep purple personal knowledge base.

### Key Features
- **Auto-Saving**: Content updates save in real-time.
- **Wikilinks**: Click [[Architecture]] or [[Ideas]] to navigate or create notes.
- **Tags**: Categorize thoughts using `#knowledge`, `#projects`, or `#ideas`.
- **Hybrid Search**: SQLite indexes your links and computes cosine similarity with local vector embeddings.

### Pane Resizing
Click and drag the central vertical bar between the editor and preview pane to customize your layout width.
